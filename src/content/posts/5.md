# Bookkeeping Best Practices for Small Business Success

Good bookkeeping is the foundation of sound business financial management. Whether you handle it yourself or hire a professional, following best practices ensures accurate records, tax compliance, and informed decision-making.

## Choose the Right Accounting Method

### Cash Basis Accounting

- Records transactions when cash changes hands
- Simpler for small businesses
- Best for service-based businesses with few assets

### Accrual Basis Accounting

- Records revenue when earned, expenses when incurred
- More accurate picture of financial health
- Required for businesses with inventory or over $25M in revenue

## Organize Your Records Systematically

### Chart of Accounts

Create a logical structure for categorizing transactions:

- Assets (Cash, Accounts Receivable, Inventory)
- Liabilities (Accounts Payable, Loans)
- Equity (Owner's Equity, Retained Earnings)
- Revenue (Sales, Service Income)
- Expenses (Office Supplies, Utilities, Salaries)

### File Organization

- Maintain separate folders for each tax year
- Keep receipts and invoices organized by category
- Use consistent naming conventions for digital files
- Back up records regularly (cloud and physical storage)

## Daily Bookkeeping Habits

### Record Transactions Promptly

- Enter transactions within 24-48 hours
- Categorize expenses correctly
- Reconcile bank accounts weekly
- Review financial reports monthly

### Maintain Supporting Documentation

- Keep all receipts and invoices
- Note business purpose for expenses
- Document business miles and use
- Preserve bank statements and canceled checks

## Essential Bookkeeping Tasks

### Bank Reconciliation

- Compare your records with bank statements monthly
- Identify and correct discrepancies
- Catch bank errors and unauthorized transactions
- Ensure all deposits and withdrawals are recorded

### Accounts Receivable Management

- Invoice customers promptly
- Follow up on overdue payments
- Track aging receivables
- Write off bad debts when necessary

### Accounts Payable Management

- Pay bills on time to avoid late fees
- Take advantage of early payment discounts
- Track vendor payment terms
- Maintain good supplier relationships

### Inventory Tracking (if applicable)

- Use FIFO (First In, First Out) or specific identification
- Conduct regular physical inventory counts
- Adjust for shrinkage and obsolescence
- Maintain accurate cost of goods sold

## Leverage Technology

### Accounting Software

- QuickBooks, Xero, or Wave for small businesses
- Automated transaction categorization
- Real-time financial reporting
- Mobile access for on-the-go entry

### Digital Tools

- Receipt scanning apps (Expensify, Zoho Expense)
- Bank feed integration
- Automated invoice generation
- Cloud storage for document management

## Quarterly and Annual Tasks

### Quarterly Reviews

- Review profit and loss statements
- Analyze expense trends
- Update financial projections
- Prepare for quarterly tax payments

### Year-End Preparation

- Reconcile all accounts
- Review inventory valuations
- Prepare for tax filing
- Plan for the upcoming year

## When to Seek Professional Help

Consider outsourcing bookkeeping when:

- Your business grows beyond your capacity
- You need specialized industry knowledge
- Tax complexity increases
- You want to focus on core business activities

## Common Bookkeeping Mistakes to Avoid

- Mixing personal and business expenses
- Delaying transaction recording
- Inconsistent categorization
- Neglecting reconciliation
- Poor record retention practices

## The Payoff

Proper bookkeeping provides:

- Accurate financial statements for decision-making
- Smooth tax filing and compliance
- Better cash flow management
- Clear business valuation for loans or sales
- Reduced risk of audits and penalties

Invest time in good bookkeeping habits now, and your business will reap the rewards of financial clarity and control.
