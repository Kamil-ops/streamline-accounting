## Business Valuation & Appraisal Services

Understanding your business's true economic value is crucial for strategic decision-making, whether you're planning a sale, seeking investment, or navigating legal proceedings. Our certified business valuation experts provide comprehensive appraisals using industry-standard methodologies and years of experience.

### Valuation Methodologies

**Income Approach**

- Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) analysis
- Capitalized earnings methods
- Excess earnings valuation
- Multi-period excess earnings method

**Market Approach**

- Comparable company analysis
- Precedent transactions analysis
- Guideline public company comparisons
- Industry-specific valuation multiples

**Asset-Based Approach**

- Adjusted net asset value
- Liquidation value analysis
- Going concern asset valuation
- Intangible asset identification and valuation
