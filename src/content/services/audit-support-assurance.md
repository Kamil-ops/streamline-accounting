## Audit Support & Assurance Services

Navigating audits can be complex and stressful, but with our expert audit support services, you'll face regulatory examinations with confidence. We prepare your financial records, strengthen internal controls, and ensure compliance with accounting standards and regulatory requirements.

### Comprehensive Audit Preparation

**Documentation & Organization**

- Complete financial record review and organization
- Supporting documentation compilation
- Audit trail establishment and verification
- Financial statement preparation and review

**Internal Control Assessment**

- Control environment evaluation
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategies
- Control activity testing and improvements
- Monitoring and oversight procedures

### Types of Audits We Support

**Financial Statement Audits**

- GAAP compliance assurance
- Revenue recognition verification
- Asset and liability validation
- Equity transaction review

**Compliance Audits**

- Regulatory requirement adherence
- Industry-specific compliance checks
- Government grant and funding audits
- Non-profit organization audits

**Internal Audits**

- Operational efficiency reviews
- Fraud prevention assessments
- Process improvement recommendations
- Risk management strategies

### Our Audit Support Process

1. **Pre-Audit Planning**: Review audit scope, timeline, and requirements
2. **Documentation Preparation**: Organize and prepare all necessary records
3. **Control Testing**: Evaluate and strengthen internal controls
4. **Audit Facilitation**: Support auditors throughout the examination process
5. **Finding Resolution**: Address audit findings and implement improvements
6. **Post-Audit Review**: Analyze results and recommend ongoing improvements
