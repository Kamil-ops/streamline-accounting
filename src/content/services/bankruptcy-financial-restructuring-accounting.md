## Bankruptcy & Financial Restructuring Services

When businesses face financial distress, our specialized bankruptcy and restructuring services provide the expert guidance needed to navigate complex proceedings and emerge stronger. We work closely with business owners, creditors, and legal counsel to achieve optimal outcomes.

### Comprehensive Bankruptcy Support

**Chapter 11 Reorganization**

- Debtor-in-possession (DIP) financing arrangements
- Cash flow projections and liquidity analysis
- Creditor claim verification and objection
- Plan of reorganization development

**Chapter 7 Liquidation**

- Asset valuation and liquidation planning
- Creditor distribution optimization
- Tax implications analysis
- Wind-down accounting and reporting

### Financial Restructuring Expertise

**Balance Sheet Restructuring**

- Debt restructuring and renegotiation
- Equity restructuring and recapitalization
- Asset sales and monetization strategies
- Capital structure optimization

**Operational Restructuring**

- Cost reduction and efficiency improvements
- Revenue enhancement strategies
- Working capital management
- Cash flow stabilization plans

### Turnaround Financial Management

**Crisis Management**

- Immediate liquidity assessment and stabilization
- Emergency cash flow forecasting
- Critical vendor and creditor negotiations
- Short-term survival strategies

**Recovery Planning**

- Long-term financial projections
- Operational improvement plans
- Growth strategy development
- Exit planning and valuation

### Our Process

1. **Initial Assessment**: Comprehensive financial analysis and viability evaluation
2. **Strategy Development**: Customized restructuring or bankruptcy strategy
3. **Stakeholder Communication**: Transparent communication with creditors and stakeholders
4. **Implementation**: Guided execution of restructuring or bankruptcy proceedings
5. **Recovery Support**: Post-restructuring financial management and monitoring
