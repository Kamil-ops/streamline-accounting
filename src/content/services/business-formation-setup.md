## Business Formation & Entity Setup Services

Starting a new business involves critical decisions that impact your future success, tax obligations, and legal protections. Our business formation services guide you through every step of establishing your company, ensuring you choose the right structure and complete all necessary registrations.

### Choosing the Right Entity Type

We help you evaluate different business structures including:

- **LLC (Limited Liability Company)**: Offers liability protection with pass-through taxation
- **Corporation (C-Corp/S-Corp)**: Best for businesses planning to seek venture capital or go public
- **Partnership**: Suitable for businesses with multiple owners
- **Sole Proprietorship**: Simplest form but offers no liability protection

### Complete Formation Package

**Legal Documentation**

- Articles of Organization/Incorporation
- Operating Agreements and Bylaws
- Initial meeting minutes and resolutions

**Government Filings**

- State business registration and fees
- EIN (Employer Identification Number) from IRS
- Business licenses and permits
- Sales tax registration

**Compliance Setup**

- Initial tax elections and filings
- Registered agent services
- Business bank account setup guidance

### Why Professional Formation Matters

**Asset Protection**
Proper entity formation separates your personal assets from business liabilities, protecting your personal finances.

**Tax Optimization**
Different entity types offer various tax advantages. We help you choose the structure that best fits your financial goals.

**Funding Readiness**
The right entity structure makes it easier to attract investors, secure loans, and position your business for growth.

**Compliance Confidence**
We ensure all filings are completed correctly and on time, avoiding costly penalties and legal issues.
